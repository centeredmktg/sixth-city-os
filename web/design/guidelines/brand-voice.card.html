We sound like this
  • Speak to “you,” as “we.” Direct and warm.
  • Sentence case. Short, declarative. “We help you move.”
  • Frame AI as a quiet enabler of depth and speed.
  • Rule of three: inaccessible, inefficient, incomplete.
Not like this
  • Hype, exclamation, fear-based pressure.
  • Corporate third-person — “users,” “clients.”
  • Jargon: synergy, leverage, unlock value.
  • Emoji, or AI as the hero of the sentence.
